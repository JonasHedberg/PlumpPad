Privacy Policy

How PlumpPad handles information in the app and what happens when you visit this website.

Last updated: October 27, 2026

Responsible developer

PlumpPad is developed and provided by Jonas Hedberg. For app or privacy questions, use PlumpPad on GitHub Issues. No direct email address is published on this website.

Information stored in the app

The app may store player names you enter, seating order, dealer, bids, tricks taken, scores, missed bids (“plumps”), game state, dates and results. Active games are saved automatically. Completed games can be retained in optional history. Saved player names and preferences are also stored locally.

This information is used to calculate scores, display and resume games, show results and prepare reports. No account is required.

Named custom house rule sets and their options (for scoring, deals and player limits) are also stored locally. A selected rule set may be stored as part of a saved game.

Data collection and third parties

The reviewed app version does not transmit game information to Jonas Hedberg or a developer-operated server. It has no advertising, third-party analytics or tracking. App data is not automatically sold or shared with advertisers or analytics providers.

This statement applies to the reviewed version; this policy will be updated if a future version changes its data practices.

Printing, exporting and backups

When you choose to print a PDF score sheet or share/export a CSV file, PlumpPad uses Apple’s system interfaces. The recipient, printer or service you choose may process the file under its own terms. The app never submits reports automatically.

Locally stored app data may be included in normal device backups, depending on iOS and iCloud settings. Exported files and copies in other apps must be managed where you saved or shared them.

Retention and deletion

Active games remain until completed or deleted. Completed games are retained when history was enabled at completion until removed. Saved players remain until deleted. App preferences remain until changed or the app’s data is removed.

You can delete saved players and completed-game history under Settings, remove games from the game list, and remove the app to delete its local data from the device. Backups and exported copies may need separate attention. See Privacy choices and deletion.

This website and GitHub

This site is hosted with GitHub Pages. It does not include its own analytics cookies, third-party ads or tracking scripts. GitHub may process technical connection data when delivering the site under its Privacy Statement.

When you open or create a GitHub Issue, GitHub processes the information you provide. Issues and comments can be public. Please do not post sensitive information or private player details.

Questions and rights

Because game information is not received by the developer, it generally cannot be accessed, exported or erased remotely by the developer. Manage your information on your device. For privacy questions, you may open an issue in the PlumpPad repository without posting sensitive details publicly.
