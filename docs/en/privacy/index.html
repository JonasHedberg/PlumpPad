Privacy Policy

This policy applies to PlumpPad for iPhone and iPad. Last updated September 14, 2026.

In short: PlumpPad requires no account and the app does not send game data, player names, analytics data or identifiers to the developer or to an analytics service.

1. What data is processed in the app?

You can enter player names and create game results. PlumpPad stores the following information locally in the app’s storage on your device so its features can work:

  • player names that you enter or choose to save,
  • active games, including bids, tricks taken, scores, plumps, dealer and round status,
  • completed games when result history is enabled,
  • local preferences, such as whether result history should be retained.

The reviewed app version uses SwiftData and UserDefaults for this local storage.

2. Data collected by PlumpPad

The PlumpPad developer does not collect data from the app. The app contains no account or sign-in feature, no developer-operated network communication, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. It also contains no third-party SDKs that collect user data.

3. How data is used

Locally stored information is used only for app functionality: resuming a game, calculating and showing scores, reusing saved player names, displaying optional history and creating a printable results report.

4. Sharing and printing

PlumpPad does not share app data with the developer, advertising networks or analytics services. If you choose to print a result, the app creates a PDF locally and hands it to the iOS system printing interface. The print operation is then handled by iOS and the printer or print destination you select.

Depending on your Apple and device settings, the operating system may include app data in a device backup. This is controlled by Apple/iOS and is not a transfer initiated by or accessible to the PlumpPad developer.

5. Retention and deletion

  • Active games: retained until you continue or choose to abandon/delete the game.
  • Result history: retained only when the feature is enabled. History can be cleared in the app and individual saved results can be removed.
  • Saved players: retained until you delete them in Settings.
  • Local preferences: retained until changed or the app’s local storage is removed.

Because the developer has no server-side copy, there is no account or remote record to request deletion from. You control the local information in the app. Deleting the app normally removes its local data from the device; device backups, if any, are managed separately by Apple.

6. Consent and privacy choices

PlumpPad does not use tracking and therefore does not request App Tracking Transparency permission. You choose which names to enter and whether completed results are retained. See Privacy choices and deletion for step-by-step instructions.

7. Third parties

The reviewed app version contains no third-party SDKs. If a future version introduces services that collect or process user data, this policy and the App Store Connect disclosures will be updated before that functionality is distributed.

8. This website

This website is published with GitHub Pages. PlumpPad does not add analytics, advertising networks or tracking scripts to the website. GitHub may process technical information normally generated when serving a web page under GitHub’s own privacy statement.

9. Changes

This policy may be updated if the app’s features or data practices change. The date at the top shows when it was last updated.

10. Contact

For privacy or app questions, use the PlumpPad support page and open an issue in the PlumpPad repository on GitHub. No direct email address is published on this website.
