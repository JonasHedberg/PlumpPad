Game rules and scoring models

How a game works

PlumpPad is a scorekeeper for a card game played with physical cards. Choose the players and seating order, select the first dealer, and choose a rule set. Players bid the number of tricks they expect, play the deal according to their agreed card rules, then record tricks taken.

The app calculates the result using the rules selected when the game starts. It does not deal cards, manage trumps, or determine which physical card won a trick.

Swedish Plump – default preset

The Swedish preset uses a 52-card deck, 2–8 players and at most min(10, floor(52/player count)) cards per player. Deals go from the maximum down to one and back again, with one one-card deal per player. The dealer rotates each round.

The player after the dealer bids first and the dealer bids last. The final dealer bid may not make the sum of bids equal the number of available tricks. An exact bid earns 10 + tricks taken; a missed bid earns 0 and a plump. Highest total wins; shared top scores count as a tie.

Available scoring models

House rules can change the score calculation. “Difference” means abs(bid − tricks taken). These are the app’s supported calculation models, not necessarily universal national rules.

  • Exact bid or zero: bonus + tricks for an exact bid, otherwise 0; a zero bid may have its own bonus.
  • Tricks plus bonus: tricks always count, with an extra bonus for an exact bid.
  • Difference penalty: exact earns bonus + tricks; missed bids lose one point per trick difference.
  • Double difference: exact earns bonus + 2 × tricks; missed bids lose twice the difference.
  • Five-times: exact earns bonus + 5 × tricks; a miss earns −5 × difference.
  • Squared: exact earns bonus + bid²; a miss earns −difference².
  • 7-truf: exact positive bid earns 10 × bid, exact zero earns 5 × cards, otherwise the number of tricks taken.
  • Clag: additional special scoring in deals 9 and 15.

Customize and save house rules

In New Game, choose a country or variant, then open House rules. You can adjust minimum/maximum player counts, deck size, maximum hand, deal direction, repeated minimum/maximum deals, bidding method, the final-bid restriction, and scoring style with bonuses. Save a named rule set or choose a saved set when starting another game.

Bidding, turn order and last bid

For turn-by-turn entry, PlumpPad shows the next bidder. The final-bid restriction applies only when enabled in the selected profile. The settings offer a simultaneous bidding option, but the reviewed bidding screen still collects entries one player at a time; a separate hidden simultaneous-reveal workflow is not implemented.

Tricks and final results

Record tricks using the dropdown or plus/minus controls. Their sum must equal the number of tricks available in the deal before scoring can be finalized. Review standings, the complete score sheet and the rules used. Finished results can be printed as PDF or shared as CSV.

Card play and trumps

Rule presets control the score sheet and deal schedule, not physical card play. Agree on trumps, following suit and other table conventions before playing. The in-app general rules page describes basic Swedish Plump; this website also documents the newer rule-profile settings.
