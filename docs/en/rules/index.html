How to play Plump

The rules profile used by PlumpPad for bidding and scoring.

Objective

Predict exactly how many tricks you will win in each deal. The highest total score after all deals wins. Equal highest scores count as a tie.

Deck and players

Use a standard 52-card deck without jokers for 2–8 players. Ace is high, followed by king, queen, jack and 10–2. Players sit in a fixed order.

Deal schedule

The hand size begins at its maximum, decreases to one and then increases again. The maximum is min(10, floor(52 / player count)). PlumpPad uses one single-card deal per player for fair dealer rotation at the bottom of the schedule.

Bids and turn order

The dealer rotates each round. The player immediately after the dealer bids first; the dealer bids last. Bids are integers from zero to the number of cards dealt per player. The dealer must not bid a number that makes the sum of all bids exactly equal the number of available tricks. PlumpPad blocks this forbidden value.

Playing tricks

The lead player starts a trick. Other players must follow suit if able. With no trump, the highest card in the led suit wins. The winner leads the next trick. Agree on any trump variation before playing; the app records bids and tricks, but does not enforce physical card play.

Scoring and plumps

If the number of tricks won matches the bid exactly, score 10 + tricks taken. Otherwise score 0 points and record a plump. The recorded trick totals must match the number of tricks in the deal to complete it.

House rules

Plump has many house-rule variations, including trump conventions and alternative deal schedules. This page describes the scorekeeping profile actually used by the app. Agree on card-play rules before starting.
