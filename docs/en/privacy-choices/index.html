Privacy choices

Privacy choices and deletion

PlumpPad has no user account and the developer stores no server-side copy of your games. Your relevant choices are therefore made directly on the device.

Delete an active game

Go to the home screen, tap the trash button next to the active game, and confirm that you want to abandon it.

Delete result history

Open Settings and choose to clear saved history. You can also delete individual entries in History. Turn off Save result history if new completed games should not be retained.

Delete saved players

Open Settings → Saved Players and remove names you no longer want to keep.

Delete all local app data

Delete PlumpPad from the device. iOS may maintain separate device backups depending on your Apple settings; those are managed by Apple and iOS, not by PlumpPad.

Tracking and consent

PlumpPad does not use tracking and has no separate tracking setting. The reviewed version does not send app data to the developer.

Do I need to request deletion from the developer?

No for the reviewed app version: there is no account and no server-stored PlumpPad data held by the developer to delete. If the app’s data model changes in a future release, this page and the Privacy Policy will be updated.

Need help with these steps? Go to Support.
