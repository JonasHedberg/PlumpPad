Privacy choices and deletion

How to manage locally stored games, player names and history on your device.

Last updated: October 20, 2026
On this pageCompleted games and historyActive gamesSaved player namesReports and printoutsDelete all local app dataNo login or developer-side storage

Completed games and history

Open Settings → Save result history to decide whether future completed games are retained. Use Clear saved history to delete saved completed results. Turning history off does not automatically erase previously saved entries.

Active games

Active games are saved so you can resume later. Delete a game from the home screen’s game list when you no longer need it.

Saved player names

Under Settings you can delete saved players. Previous game records may still contain those names until you delete the corresponding games.

Reports and printouts

PDF printouts and CSV files are generated on your device when you choose to do so. Files saved or shared elsewhere must be managed in the receiving app, service or device.

Delete all local app data

Uninstalling PlumpPad normally removes its local data from the device. Copies in iOS backups, exported files and other apps may remain.

No login or developer-side storage

PlumpPad has no user accounts or developer-run server holding your game data. There is therefore no server-side account or game data to request deletion of. Questions can be raised via GitHub Issues (remember issues are public).
