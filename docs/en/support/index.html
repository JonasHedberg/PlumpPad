Frequently asked questions

A detailed rules guide is also included inside the app.

How do I start a game?

Tap New Game, choose 2–8 players, enter names or select saved players, confirm seating order and choose the first dealer.

How do I resume a paused game?

Active games are automatically saved on the device. Open the game on the home screen to resume.

Why is a dealer bid missing?

The dealer bids last and cannot make the sum of all bids equal the number of tricks in the deal.

How do I enter tricks taken?

After bidding, choose each player’s tricks taken from the menu. The total must equal the tricks available in that deal.

How are points calculated?

An exact bid earns 10 plus the number of tricks taken. A missed bid scores zero and counts as a plump.

Can I reuse player names?

Yes. Add saved players under Settings and choose them when creating a game.

How do I delete saved results?

Open Settings and select Clear saved history. The history setting does not affect active games.

Can I print or export?

Yes. The score sheet and final results offer A4/Letter PDF printing and CSV sharing through iOS.

How do I change language?

PlumpPad supports Swedish and English and follows your device or per-app language selection in iOS Settings.

Do I need internet?

No, scorekeeping works offline. GitHub support and this website require an internet connection.
