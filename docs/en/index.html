Built for the card table

The essentials, without the clutter

♣

Correct turn order

The app follows the dealer and bidding order and prevents the final bid from making the bid total equal the number of available tricks.

0–8

2–8 players

Enter names directly or reuse saved players. Active games are stored locally so you can continue later.

✓

Automatic scoring

An exact bid scores 10 + tricks. A missed bid scores 0 and adds a plump. Results can be printed on A4 or Letter.

Privacy

Your game night stays yours

The reviewed app version contains no networking code, advertising, analytics or tracking. Game data, saved players and preferences are stored in the app’s local storage on the device.
