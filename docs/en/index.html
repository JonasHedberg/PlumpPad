Features

Everything at the card table

Real cards, digital scorekeeping. Choose a rule preset before starting a new game.

1

Choose your rules

Select from 12 rule presets, including Swedish Plump, Oh Hell and Piratbridge.

2

Custom, reusable house rules

Adjust deals, deck, player limits, final-bid rules and scoring. Save rule sets for future games.

3

Bidding and dealer order

See the dealer and next bidder. The final-bid restriction applies when the chosen rules enable it.

4

Tricks and automatic scores

Record tricks with plus/minus buttons or a dropdown. Scores follow the selected rules.

5

Pause and resume

Active games are saved on-device; reusable players and optional result history remain available.

6

Print and CSV export

Print landscape PDFs on A4/Letter or share a CSV result with apps such as Numbers or Excel.

7

Display preferences

Keep the display awake during a game and optionally dim after inactivity.

8

12 languages

The app interface and help follow the iOS language selection.

Your table, your rules

Start from a preset, customize scoring and deal schedules, then save the configuration under a name for future games.
